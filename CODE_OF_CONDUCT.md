# Code of conduct

We want QQ's community to be useful and pleasant for everyone who takes part,
whatever their background or experience.

## Expected

- Be direct and kind. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- Give credit; take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep discussions on topic and reviews focused on the change.

## Not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualised content,
publishing others' private information, sustained disruption, or retaliation
against anyone who reports a problem. This applies in issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other space connected to the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub
(<https://github.com/retsu-AI/qq/security/advisories/new> reaches them
confidentially even when the report is not a security issue) or by email to
the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn, or ban as they
judge proportionate, and will explain their decision to the people involved.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, shortened.
